## Notes

Kodak DCS Photo Desk 4.3 supports Kodak DCS Pro cameras including the DCS Pro 14n, DCS Pro SLR/n and DCS Pro SLR/c.

Support for some older Kodak DCS models was removed compared to Photo Desk 4.2.
